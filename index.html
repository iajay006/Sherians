Amiphoria 2026

Amiphoria is the massive annual cultural and sports festival held at Amity University Jharkhand in Ranchi, a multi-day event featuring over 40 events, attracting thousands from various colleges for a fusion of academic, tech, sports, and entertainment activities like music, dance, gaming, and fashion shows, culminating in performances by famous artists. It's a platform for students to showcase talent, with recent editions including names like DJ Lil B, DJ Swizel, and singer Asees Kaur performing.
